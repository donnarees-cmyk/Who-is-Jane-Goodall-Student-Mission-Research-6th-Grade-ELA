      CASE BRIEFING

      Your mission: Build a booklet that explains who Jane Goodall is, using reliable sources and your own words.

      There is no timer. Careful research is better than fast research. Take the time to get it right.

      How this booklet works

      1. 📄 Choose your sources. Find at least two and check that you can trust them.
      2. 🗂️ Open each case file. Every page has a mission and the evidence to collect.
      3. ✍️ Write facts in your own words and tell which source each one came from.
      4. 🖼️ Add pictures. Draw a sketch, or add a photo and tell where it came from.
      5. ⏳ Build a timeline of important events.
      6. ✅ Check and turn in. Take screenshots, or save a PDF, and upload it to Canvas.

      Case rules

      • 🔎 Every fact needs a source.
      • ✏️ Use your own words. Do not copy.
      • ⚖️ Two sources are better than one.
      • 🤔 Sources can disagree. If they do, write what each one says. That is good research.

      Writing with your Apple Pencil

      You can type in any box. You can also write in a box with your Apple Pencil. If Scribble is on, the iPad changes your handwriting into typed words. Write big and clear, and check what appears. The sketch boxes are for drawing and labeling.

      🔊 Tap the speaker to hear any prompt or any of your own notes read aloud. Your work saves on this iPad.

      Not sure what to do? Follow these steps

      1. Choose two sources. Tap 📄 Sources at the top. Tap Use as my source on Britannica Kids (Students level). Then choose one more from a different place.
      2. Open a site. Tap Open site. It opens in a new tab. Look at the headings first.
      3. Read one small part. Then look away and say it to yourself in your own words.
      4. Come back to this tab and open a case file, like Early Life. Type one fact. Pick which source it came from.
      5. Check it. Look for the same fact in your second source. Do they agree? If they do not, write what each one says in “Do your sources disagree?”
      6. Stuck? Try the Kids level, tap 🔊 to hear a prompt, or ask your teacher.

      Where can I look?

      CASE FILE · SOURCES

      Mission: Detectives check their sources first. Choose sources you can trust.

      Add the sources you will use, then check each one. You need at least two reliable sources.

      Is it reliable?

      Check these four things. A source that passes at least three is a good choice.

      • ✍️ A named author or an expert organization wrote it.
      • 📅 It is up to date.
      • 🔗 It lists sources or links to studies.
      • 📚 It is trying to teach you, not sell you something or get clicks.

      CASE FILE · TIMELINE

      Mission: Put the evidence in order. Add at least important events. The page sorts them for you.

      Take your time. Pick events that matter, and tell which source each one came from.
